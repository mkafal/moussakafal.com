04 / Image language

Use tangible materials, light and energy to make the relationship between infrastructure and intelligence visible. The signature conductor is an original real-time 3D sculpture of interlaced copper and titanium ribbons with traveling light pulses. A conceptual still image provides an immediate fallback. Pair it with real portraiture and clearly illustrative infrastructure diagrams.

Portraiture: neutral background, confident expression, monochrome by default with a restrained color reveal. Keep the original portrait’s identity intact.

All continuous motion can be paused. The 3D scene stops rendering when it is offscreen or the tab is hidden; mobile rendering uses reduced geometry and resolution. Honor the visitor’s reduced-motion preference. Avoid flashing, autoplay audio, scroll hijacking and animations that block reading.

06 / Composition & voice

Editorial, spacious, deliberate.

Alternate immersive dark sections with clear light reading surfaces. Use oversized section numbers, thin rules and asymmetry. Reserve rounded shapes for interactive controls.

Technical credibility. Human clarity.

Connect the technology to a decision and a customer outcome. Use examples with clear scope. Publish performance figures, client names and endorsements only when confirmed and cleared.
